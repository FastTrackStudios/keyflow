# Alternatives

Keyflow is not an engraving program. It has no manual control and will not gain any.

[MuseScore](https://musescore.org) and [LilyPond](https://lilypond.org) are engraving programs, and good ones. Keyflow aims to stand alongside them, not replace them.

## Which to use

| You want | Use |
|---|---|
| A master rhythm chart, fast | Keyflow |
| A slur moved three points left | MuseScore |
| Typeset scores from source | LilyPond |

## The way out

Keyflow exports SVG. Take it into a graphics editor and edit it directly — that is the escape hatch for anything the format will not do.

> [!note]- Where it is going
> Whole orchestral parts, eventually. Precise manual engraving stays out of scope even then.

> [!note]- On the name
> [[lilypond-rhythm|LilyPond Rhythm Notation]] borrows LilyPond's way of *writing* durations — `_2` for a half note. It is a syntax, not the program.
