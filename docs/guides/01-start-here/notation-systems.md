# Letters, Numbers, or Numerals

Musicians have developed many different ways of communciating the same
information, each with their own stregnths and weaknesses. Keyflow allows three
main ways of notating pitch or root notes of chords, and is open to more styles
if they are requested.

````tabs
=== Letters
```kf+
#G
VS 4
G C Em D
```

The pitches themselves. What people say out loud, and what a dep wants
to see.
=== Numbers
```kf+
#G
VS 4
1 4 6m 5
```

Positions in the key. Change `#G` and every chord moves with it.
=== Roman numerals
```kf+
#G
VS 4
I IV vi V
```

Positions again. The case carries the quality: `vi` is minor because it
is lowercase.
````
