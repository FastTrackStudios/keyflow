- To show how to add chord diagrams, we'll use this well-known song.



+
: Ex. 2  Love Me Tender /Aura Lee  Elvis
-
= verse
D E7 . A7 D
D E7 A7 D 
 
= chorus
D_F#7/C# Bm _ D7/A G _ Gm6  D
D_B7 E7 A7 D

TAB1
- To add a chord diagram, you need to put a period after the chord. 
- So I wrote “E7 .” on the first line.

+
: Ex. 3  Love Me Tender /Aura Lee  Elvis
-
= verse
D E7 . A7 . D
D E7 . A7 . D 
 
= chorus
D_F#7/C# Bm _ D7/A G _ Gm6  D
D_B7 . E7 . A7 . D

TAB1
- Here I added a period after all dominant seventh chords.

- For convenience, you can add a period before the line and a chord diagram 
- will be added to each chord on that line.
- Here I added a period before the first line.

+
: Ex. 5  Love Me Tender /Aura Lee  Elvis
-

. D E7 A7 D

. D_F#7/C# Bm _ D7/A G _ Gm6  D
. D_B7 E7 A7 D

TAB1
- Here I started each line with a “.” 
- Therefore, chord diagrams are displayed throughout.
- But you probably notice that some chord diagrams are missing ...

- Different voicings
// D' x57775x12341

- Sometimes you want to specify different voicings of the same chord within a song.
- You can do this by adding an apostrophe to differentiate between D and D'.
+
: Ex. 8  Love Me Tender /Aura Lee  Elvis
-
-
= verse
D. E7. A7. D
D E7 A7 D 
 
= chorus
. D'_F#7/C#. Bm. _ D7/A. G. _ Gm6.  D
D_B7. E7 A7 D
