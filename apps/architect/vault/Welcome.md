# Keyflow

This is the development vault: `devenv up` (or `just app`) seeds it from
`apps/architect/vault` in the keyflow repo the first time it runs, into
devenv's state folder. Edits here stay in that copy, not in the repo.

- `Master Rhythm/`: the Master Rhythm charts (Christmas set and others).
- `JT Recital/`: JT's senior recital set, in show order.
- `Charts/`: examples from the chordsheet corpus.

Open a chart to see it engraved.
